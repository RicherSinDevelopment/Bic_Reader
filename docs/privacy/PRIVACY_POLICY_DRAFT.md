## Information we collect

### Account and authentication information

When you create or use an account, we process your name, email address, account identifier, authentication credentials or tokens, and information returned by Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. The Google Sign-In SDK may also process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network or device signals, usage information, and other technical service data for authentication functionality and analytics. Password authentication is provided by Supabase; we do not store a readable copy of your password.

### Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments. We and RevenueCat receive subscription and transaction information needed to offer purchases, restore purchases, determine entitlement status, and provide premium features. We do not receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

### PDFs, annotations, and reading information

PDFs are stored on your device. When premium cloud sync is enabled, we upload PDFs and related metadata to Supabase, including filenames, file details, highlights, notes, reading position, completion percentage, last-opened time, reader settings, and appearance preferences. This information is associated with your account so it can sync across your devices.

### AI reading assistant

When you choose to use the AI assistant, Bic Reader sends your question and the PDF text selected for context to a Supabase Edge Function, which sends it to OpenAI to generate an answer. Do not submit confidential or highly sensitive material unless you are comfortable having that material processed by these providers. AI conversation history is saved locally on your device. OpenAI API content is not used to train OpenAI models by default, but OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs under its applicable data controls, normally for up to 30 days. Bic Reader requests that Responses API application state not be stored by setting `store` to `false`.

### Technical information

Our service providers necessarily receive technical request information such as IP address, device or app information, timestamps, and service logs when your device communicates with them. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of the service. Bic Reader does not use this information for advertising tracking.

### Error reporting

We use Sentry to receive JavaScript errors, native crash reports, app and build versions, general device and operating-system information, and limited operational events needed to diagnose failures. Bic Reader configures Sentry not to send account identity, PDF text, filenames, file paths, annotations, AI questions or answers, screenshots, screen recordings, view hierarchies, or network request contents. We use crash information only to maintain app functionality and do not use it for tracking.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and support accounts;
- open, read, annotate, synchronize, and restore PDF libraries;
- preserve reading position and personalize reader settings;
- process subscriptions and provide premium access;
- answer user-requested AI questions;
- maintain reliability, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with law; and
- respond to privacy or support requests.

We do not sell personal information and do not use app data to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

## Service providers

We use:

- **Supabase** for authentication, hosted database, file storage, and Edge Functions;
- **RevenueCat** for subscription and entitlement management;
- **OpenAI** for the optional AI reading assistant;
- **Apple** for Sign in with Apple and App Store purchase processing; and
- **Google** for optional Google Sign-In.
- **Sentry** for privacy-filtered error and crash reporting.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices and may process it in countries other than your own.

## Retention

We retain account and synced content while your account remains active and as needed to provide the service. We may retain limited records for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or backup recovery where permitted by law. Provider logs may follow the provider's own documented retention schedule. Local PDFs, annotations, conversations, and settings remain on your device until removed by you, removed through account deletion where supported, or removed by uninstalling or clearing the app's data.

## Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to use cloud sync, social login, and the AI assistant. You can delete your account inside **Profile → Delete account**. Account deletion removes the account and associated cloud data and attempts to delete the RevenueCat customer record. It does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel billing through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact **[PRIVACY EMAIL]** to make a request. We may need to verify your identity.
